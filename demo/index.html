The craft layer

What the catalog was missing

The original skill knew what to build — archetypes, blocks, libraries, recipes. It did not carry why it looks expensive. Two sites can ship the identical component list and one reads as a studio project. The difference lives in these six files.

01

design-system.md — proportions

Modular type scales with a different ratio for body and display. The solved clamp() formula, not a guess. Tracking tables — display type over 80px takes −0.03em or it reads as amateur, every time. A non-linear 4pt spacing ramp. OKLCH ramps with peak chroma in the middle and tinted neutrals. Optical alignment. Nested-radius math. Layered shadows with one committed light direction.

02

liquid-glass.md — material

What Liquid Glass actually is: refraction, not blur. Three tiers — CSS baseline, real SVG feDisplacementMap lensing with chromatic aberration, and WebGL transmission. Plus the legibility problem nobody solves, and nine cousin materials so the whole web does not end up glass.

03

motion-spec.md — the numbers

Duration ladder. Easing per motion type — entrances out, exits in, exits shorter. Spring parameters mapped to feel. Stagger math with a real budget. Choreography: overlap, lead and follow, the counter-scale reveal almost nobody implements.

04

assets.md — generate before you source

Grain, mesh gradients, dither, noise textures — generated, zero dependencies, unique to the project. Then where to source everything else with licences that survive a client asking. Then the real optimization commands: sharp, ffmpeg, svgo, glyphhanger, gltf-transform. Every asset on this page was generated by scripts/gen-assets.mjs.

05

SOP.md — the procedure

Fourteen phases, five gates. Including the one that matters: do not start building until direction confidence clears 90.

06

qa-rubric.md — the standard

100 points across 10 categories, an amateur-tells hit list, and a self-critique loop that exits at 90. Without a measurable standard, "make it better" is an infinite loop.

07

kit/ — running code

Tokens, glass, generated filters, and a zero-dependency motion library on one rAF loop. This page is built from it.

Material lab

Glass is a lens, not a blur

Most glassmorphism on the web is one line: blur plus a white tint. Real glass refracts at the edge, recovers saturation the blur destroyed, and catches light asymmetrically along a curved rim. Compare the first two panels — same backdrop, same radius.

Frosted
(2019)

Naive. blur(12px) + 10% white + one flat shadow. Desaturated, flat, edge reads as a sticker.

Liquid Glass
Tier 1

Tier 1 — CSS. saturate(180%) recovers colour; asymmetric inset rim reads as a bevel; two-layer shadow. Ships everywhere.

Refraction
+ chroma

Tier 2 — SVG lens. feDisplacementMap bends the backdrop at the rim; three channels at different scales give chromatic aberration. Chromium only.

Clay

Cousin. Matte, no specular, soft double shadow. Fintech, education, wellness. Decorative only — the contrast will not carry controls.

Brushed metal

Cousin. Anisotropic highlight perpendicular to the grain. Audio hardware, watches, automotive.

Raw

Cousin. No material at all. Hairline, zero radius. The strongest counter-move when everyone else is doing glass.

The scale is the design

Display ramp at ratio 1.414, body ramp at 1.2 — one scale for both either makes the h1 timid or the caption absurd. Every step is fluid, solved as a line through two viewport points. Tracking goes negative as size climbs; leading falls the other way.

Body copy sits between 45 and 75 characters. Above 75 the eye loses its return line; below 45 the rhythm breaks. This paragraph is capped at 68ch — the single most-ignored typographic rule on the web, and one of the cheapest to obey.

Motion lab

340ms and the right curve

Motion that feels expensive is not a bigger library. It is a 340ms duration instead of 300, an ease-out instead of an ease-in-out, and a 60ms stagger instead of 100. Entrances ease out. Exits ease in, and run shorter — symmetric open/close is the loudest tell of un-designed motion.

Entrancescubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)
near-instant start, long settle

Exitscubic-bezier(.32,0,.67,0)
gentle start, accelerates away

Movescubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)
accelerate then decelerate

Landscubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)
overshoots, settles back

Scrub & looplinear
never on positional motion

Same 900ms, same distance. The curve is the entire difference.

SOP.md

Fourteen phases, five gates

The gate that matters is B. Building before you know what you are building is the largest source of wasted work, and the failure an agent is most prone to — generating plausible code is easy, knowing what to generate is not.
